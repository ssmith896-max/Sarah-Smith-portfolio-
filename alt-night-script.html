ALT 2 RECAP 11/2/26 SMITH ALT 2 intentional effect on students V/O Excitement is building at Cedarville University as students look forward to ALT Night Two this weekend. V/O With the event just around the corner, the Campus Activities Board (CAB) is preparing for a night for campus and fun. Emma Harris, a member of the board, shares a preview of what students can expect. (SOT 0.10 sec) “Two things I’m super excited about, which are new this year, are the s’mores that we have set up and the trunk-or-treat. So the s’mores are going to be on little mini bistro tables with mini fires, and so people can roast marshmallows with their friends over mini campfires. And then the trunk-or-treat is done by the orgs and SGA. And I think that will be a super fun time to bring more people together with the orgs and with SGA.” V/O Between the fall weather and this weekend’s activities, Cedarville students have plenty to look forward to.
